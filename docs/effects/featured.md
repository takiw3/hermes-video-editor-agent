# Featured effects

Generated by `scripts/build_catalog.py` from `skills/video-effects-core/effects-library/featured.json`, which is edited by hand. The editor reaches for these first: `fx.py list --featured`.

Reviewed 2026-10-29. Every verified picture effect was rendered at 1080x1920, 30 fps, at its default parameters, on two real vertical talking-head clips (and on a synthetic green screen for keying), and judged at three moments. These 50 read clearly on a phone, keep faces and captions intact, and look finished at their defaults. The rest stay verified and usable; they were too subtle at default on this footage, too niche, or a stronger version of the same job is featured.

Every featured effect is verified (all 12 checks) and not limited. The build fails otherwise. A `warn` in the flash check column means fast motion flipped high-contrast edges in a small area, with no flash at the default setting; the flash check still runs on every real render.
