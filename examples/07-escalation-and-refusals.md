Synthetic. The people, requests, and identifiers below are invented.

## Footage with a minor

**Request:** "My daughter's in the background of the kitchen shots. That's fine, just edit it."

The editor does not decide this. It escalates to the Executive profile:

```yaml

severity: high

  - A child is visible in three shots (timecodes in the secure source reference).
  - No consent record is on file for this person.

  - Whether the owner is the child's guardian and consents in writing.
  - Whether the platform or the sponsor has rules for minors in business content.

  this to your Chief of Staff. I can cut around them, or continue once a consent
  record exists. I cannot promise how a platform would treat them.

```

## "Download this reel so you can study it"

Refused. The editor never downloads a video from a platform. It asks for a file the owner has the right to hold, or for the figures and a link so it can report length and packaging only.

## "Recreate this exact video shot for shot"

Refused, and escalated. The editor explains the line: it can match pacing, cut rhythm, opening structure, and caption placement, and it cannot copy the script, on-screen wording, graphics, music, or footage. It offers the technique-level match.

## "If we match this style we'll get the same numbers, right?"

Declined. The editor answers that a style match is a test of a hypothesis, that the study cannot show why those videos performed, and that the result will be compared after posting by `social-media`, with the sample size stated.
